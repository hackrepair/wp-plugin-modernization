# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and harassment-free. Welcome people regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint. Discuss the work, evidence, and tradeoffs without personal attacks.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Giving and accepting specific, respectful feedback.
- Acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes openly.
- Protecting private source code, customer data, and security reports.
- Focusing disagreements on observable behavior and project goals.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, threats, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or sharing non-public plugin source without authorization.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to Jim Walker through the contact options at https://hackrepair.com/. Do not include private personal details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
